No bank had public pages to check for BCB, BBCI, Great Lakes Bank (GLB), BCAB, BIJE or BHB. I found no evidence that any Burundi bank offers fully digital, staff-on-tablet onboarding, apart from CRDB's agents doing instant opening.

**Takeaways:**
- The common evidence set is: ID (CNI, passport, driving licence), 2 photos, proof of address (various accepted types), and NIF plus trade register (RC) for traders.
- Products are differentiated by segment: women, diaspora, salary, scholar/student, minors.
- Agent-based instant opening (CRDB) and diaspora remote opening (BGF) are the main competitive differentiators.

## 3. Regulation
**AML/CFT law**
- Law No. 1/02 of 4 Feb 2008 on AML/CFT:
  - Art. 4: identify the client with a probative document when entering a business relationship and when opening an account. This also applies to occasional transactions of BIF 20 million or more, wire transfers, suspicion, or doubt about the data.
  - Art. 5: PEPs require senior management approval, establishing source of wealth and funds, and enhanced ongoing monitoring.
  - Source: https://bwcimplementation.org/sites/default/files/resource/Burundi-Loi-2008-lutte-blanchiment-et-financement-du-terrorisme.pdf
- **Amended by Law No. 1/08 of 27 March 2025**. It is cited in the BRB's 2026 regulation (https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2026-01/Reglement%20March%C3%A9%20Interbancaire%20BIF.pdf). Full text: https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2026-02/loi%20antiblanchiment%202025.pdf

**BRB regulation and circulars**
- BRB AML Regulation of 28.4.2023, Art. 4–5:
  - KYC/CDD procedures, identification of the **beneficial owner**, enhanced controls for PEPs, and screening against sanctions lists (notably the UN's).
  - These checks apply at onboarding and throughout the relationship.
  - **Accounts without expressly named holders are prohibited.**
  - Source: https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2024-08/Reglement%20anti-blanchiment%2028.4.2023.pdf
- **New in 2026:** BRB Regulation No. 02/2026 on AML/CFT, plus:
  - **Circular No. 01/LBC-FT/2026** on identification, identity verification and KYC (https://www.brb.bi/sites/default/files/2026-02/circulaire%20no1.pdf). The PDF is a scan and its text could not be extracted, so the details need to be read manually.
  - Circular No. 05/LBC-FT/2026 on the sanctions matrix.
  - Both are listed at https://www.brb.bi/textes_legaux_relatifs_au_secteur_bancaire. **Compliance must provide or confirm the full requirements**: document list, retention period, risk classes.

**Banking law**
- Law No. 1/17 of 22 Aug 2017 governing banking activities (cited in the BRB 2026 regulation above).

**Data protection**
- **Law No. 1/03 of 10 March 2026** on personal data protection (https://arct.gov.bi/2026/03/18/loi-n1-03-du-10-mars-2026-portant-protection-des-donnees-a-caractere-personnel/ ; https://app.maathis.com/droit/burundi/loi/1-03-loi-n103-10-mars-2026-portant-protection-donnees-caractere-personnel).
- It creates an independent authority with criminal penalties (https://cadeproject.org/updates/burundi-adopts-data-protection-law/).
- Implementing texts are still to come (https://assemblee.bi/?p=31493).
- Detailed obligations (consent, cross-border transfer, breach notification) were not verified. The DPO/Legal team should review them.
- A biometric national ID card is being piloted (assemblee.bi above). Future ID verification against it may become possible.

## 4. Oracle FLEXCUBE UBS 14.0 integration
- **Gateway web services (SOAP/XML, also available over HTTP/JMS)** are the documented way to create records.
  - Envelope: FCUBS_HEADER (SOURCE, UBSCOMP, MSGID, USERID, BRANCH, MODULEID, SERVICE, OPERATION) + FCUBS_BODY.
  - Message patterns: FSFS / IOPK / IOFS.
  - Example: CREATECUSTACC_IOPK_REQ.
  - Source: https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E88855_01/html/GW/GW04_Message_Formats.htm ; library: https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E88855_01/webservice.htm
- **Customer (CIF):** `FCUBSCustomerService` (endpoint `FCUBSCustomerService/FCUBSCustomerService`), with operations CreateCustomer / ModifyCustomer (function STDCIF / STGCIF).
  - Sources: https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/financial-services/banking-branch/14.8.0.0.0/intgu/direct-access.html ; https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E51527_01/PDF/Extensibility/FCUBS-FD02-03-01-RAD%20Web%20Service%20Development.pdf
  - Note: the exact CreateCustomer XSD for 14.0 must be taken from the bank's installed XSD set.
- **CASA account:** `FCUBSAccService` / `CreateCustAcc`. Related services:
  - TD: CreateTDCustAcc
  - Cheque book: CreateCheckBook
  - Product (account class) lists: FCUBSSTService SummaryQueryAccClasMaint / QueryAccClasMaint
  - Sources: https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/financial-services/banking-originations-cloud/14.7.3.0.0/oocfg/maintenance-oracle-banking-origination.html ; https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/financial-services/banking-branch/14.7.1.0.0/intgu/maintain-external-system-functions.html
- **REST in FCUBS** is lightweight and mainly GET-only (customer/accounts query, balances, FX rates), using headers userId, password, msgId, branch, source, entity. Source (14.7.5 guide): https://docs.oracle.com/cd/G14514_01/PDF/RestService/Oracle_Flexcube_Restful_Services_Usage.pdf
  - **So in 14.0, creation goes through the SOAP Gateway.** Use REST only for duplicate-customer lookups and reading FX and product data.
- **Setup and design notes:**
  - Register the external system (GWDEXSYS) and its allowed functions (GWDEXFUN) per operation (RAD guide above).
  - Maker-checker: Gateway records can come in unauthorised and then be authorised in FCUBS. This should be confirmed with the bank's FCUBS team.
  - OBAPI and Oracle Banking Origination would be extra licensed products (not verified whether FinBank has them).

## 5. Best practices to adopt (synthesis)
1. **Offline-first tablet capture**:
   - Encrypted local queue and synchronisation.
   - Push to FCUBS only after approval, with idempotent MSGIDs.
   - If the API fails, fall back to a pre-filled printable record (meets the user's requirement).
2. **Configurable product catalogue**, kept in sync with FCUBS account classes. Seed it with FinBank's current/savings terms plus segment products seen at competitors:
   - salary (no deposit, as at CRDB)
   - women's savings (as BANCOBU KIRA MAMA)
   - youth/minor with a birth extract (as Interbank)
   - diaspora remote (as BGF)
   - term deposit
   - SME/trader (NIF + RC)
3. **Configurable document matrix by product and customer type.** Accept all of these as proof of address: residence certificate, cahier de ménage, lease, REGIDESO bill under 3 months old.
4. **Paper signature, then scan:** generate a pre-filled form with a QR code / barcode linking it to the application. Uploading the scanned signed form becomes a mandatory gate before approval. Push the specimen signature to FCUBS if the bank supports it.
5. **AML API screening** at capture and again before approval, for all parties (holders, directors, beneficial owners, signatories). PEP cases must go to senior management approval (Law 1/02 Art. 5), which maps onto the MD/CEO tier.
6. **Duplicate check against FCUBS** (REST customer query) before creating a CIF. Respect the ban on anonymous accounts.
7. **Approval chain** clerk → branch head → Ops/Compliance (edge cases) → MD/CEO (exceptions or PEPs), with configurable rules.
8. **Data protection by design** (Law 1/03 of 2026): consent capture, encryption on device and server, retention per BRB rules, access logs, a device lock (PIN auto-lock as in the FinBank QMS design).
9. **Agent mode** for field staff (like CRDB TURI HOSE): GPS/time stamp, limited capture-only role, sync status.
10. **UI:** trilingual FR/EN/Kirundi, following the FinBank QMS design system (see ui-system.md). Confirm the brand palette.
